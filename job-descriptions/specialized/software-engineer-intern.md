## About the Role

Invitation Homes set out to solve a real problem: home leasing options were limited and the experience for renters was inconsistent.
Today, as the nation's largest single-family home rental company, our engineering teams build the technology at the center of that solution: personalized resident experiences, service tools, and the operational systems that residents and associates depend on every day.
When we ship, people feel it.

Our paid summer internship is a first step toward a career in software engineering.
You'll join a real team for about ten weeks, work on a scoped project, and ship something that matters.
Along the way you'll have a manager and an assigned mentor who are invested in your growth, plus intern events, community outreach, and an executive speaker series.

This role maps to the learning side of what many organizations call an entry-level Software Engineer.
You're a hands-on learner from day one.
The goal is to leave with real experience, real relationships, and a clear sense of what engineering looks like here.

## What You'll Do

**Technical Leadership**

- Work on a well-scoped project that contributes to your team's goals, with support from your mentor.
- Write code, fix bugs, and ship small changes to production.
- Follow your team's established patterns and conventions; ask when you're unsure why they exist.
- Join standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives as a full member of the team.

**Knowledge & Standards**

- Learn your team's core tools and technologies: what they are, why we use them, and how to use them well.
- Write automated tests alongside your code, and work with quality assurance on anything that needs manual testing.
- Treat every task as a chance to learn something new.

**Communication & Collaboration**

- Ask for help early and often.
  It's expected.
- Ask clarifying questions before diving in, and check in with your mentor regularly.
- Write clear pull request descriptions, and read your teammates' pull requests to learn from them.
- Seek out feedback and use it.
- Present what you built to your team at the end of the internship.

**Leadership & Culture**

- Show up consistently, stay curious, and be coachable.
- Take part in intern events and engineering community forums, and get to know people across the department.

## What You Bring

- A rising senior pursuing a bachelor's degree at an accredited four-year university in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- An expected graduation date between December and May following the internship.
- Foundational programming knowledge in at least one language, with a desire to build on it.
- Eagerness to learn, and openness to feedback.
- The ability to work as a self-starter and to prioritize your work with a sense of urgency.
- A willingness to collaborate with teammates and ask good questions.

## Our Stack

Our engineering teams work with TypeScript, Node.js, React, PostgreSQL, and AWS, including Lambda, DynamoDB, and RDS.
We practice CI/CD, favor infrastructure as code, and build on REST APIs and event-driven architectures.
Salesforce and Yardi are significant parts of our technology landscape: some teams build directly on these platforms, while others integrate with their APIs.
Experience with any of these is a plus, but we care more about curiosity, adaptability, and the drive to keep learning than familiarity with any specific tool.
