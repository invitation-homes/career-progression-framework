## About the Role

Invitation Homes set out to solve a real problem: home leasing options were limited and the experience for renters was inconsistent.
Today, as the nation's largest single-family home rental company, our engineering teams build the technology at the center of that solution: personalized resident experiences, service tools, and the operational systems that residents and associates depend on every day.
When we ship, people feel it.

You'll join our Financial Systems team, a trusted partner to our Accounting, Operations, Leasing, and Product teams.
The team builds and supports the systems that move money and keep our books accurate: resident payments, billing, ledgers, and the integrations that connect our property management and resident platforms.
Yardi is our system of record, and much of the work centers on building reliable software around it.
In this domain, correctness is not optional.
A defect can mean a resident is charged incorrectly or the books don't close.

We've built an engineering culture that takes that responsibility seriously: collaborative teams, high standards, and a genuine investment in the people around you.
Engineers who are supported, growing, and proud of what they ship build great software.
We're looking for a Software Engineer IV who is still a hands-on engineer at heart: writing code, making hard technical calls, and shipping.
You'll also help define what excellent looks like for the team.
This role maps to what many organizations call a tech lead or senior engineer: the point where deep technical ownership and team leadership come together.
You'll spend the majority of your time as a practitioner, with a meaningful portion invested in the people and practices around you.

From here, you'll have a clear path to Staff Engineer on the individual contributor track, or Engineering Manager if you're drawn to people leadership.

## What You'll Do

**Technical Leadership**

- Remain a hands-on engineer: write code, contribute to design, and ship alongside your team.
- Architect solutions for your team's most ambiguous and challenging problems, drawing on deep financial systems expertise.
- Design for the realities of financial transaction systems: resiliency, idempotency, reconciliation, auditability, error handling, and safe recovery from partial failures.
- Build and support integrations between Yardi, internal services, and third-party financial platforms using SOAP and REST APIs.
- Write and tune the SQL queries, stored procedures, reports, and TypeScript services that automate workflows and process transactions, with a focus on performance and reliability.
- Administer and maintain out-of-the-box Yardi capabilities, and know when configuration is the right answer instead of custom code.
- Shape the technical direction and vision for your team's systems.
- Improve team efficiency by identifying pain points and resolving them through automation, less toil, and paying down tech debt.
- Delegate low-risk technical decisions to engineers on your team, and own the high-risk ones yourself.
- Begin extending your influence beyond your team: identify opportunities to contribute to cross-team technical challenges and shared engineering practices.
- Lead and actively contribute to planning, reviews, and retrospectives as both a practitioner and a voice for engineering quality.

**Knowledge & Standards**

- Serve as an authority on the team's financial systems and on how business transactions flow through Yardi.
- Balance current delivery needs against longer-term implications, keeping the impact on other teams and on financial reporting in mind.
- Make sure your team understands engineering standards and why they exist; update guidance when those standards evolve.
- Build a culture of writing things down: in a domain this sensitive, distributed knowledge and clear documentation protect the business as much as they help the team.
- Share your expertise through Special Interest Groups (SIGs) and other cross-team forums.

**Communication & Collaboration**

- Partner closely with Accounting, Operations, Leasing, and Product to understand how the business runs and translate their needs into reliable software.
- Communicate technical concepts and risk clearly to non-technical and executive stakeholders.
- Collaborate and coordinate across teams to unblock delivery and align on direction, especially where resident-facing products depend on financial data.
- Advocate for technical solutions and build buy-in across the organization.
- Arbitrate and de-escalate technical disagreements with empathy and clarity.
- Advocate strongly for your position, then commit fully once a decision is reached, and model that behavior for others.

**Leadership & Culture**

- Mentor engineers across your team and beyond through pairing, code reviews, and coaching, working with your manager to find the right opportunities.
- Advocate for a blameless culture grounded in empathy, psychological safety, and shared accountability.
- Help improve engineering processes, especially in ways that benefit teams beyond your own.
- Contribute to hiring by conducting technical interviews and helping evaluate candidates, with a focus on a strong candidate experience.
- Hold yourself and the team to a high standard of delivery: keep momentum, keep a bias to action, and ship software that works correctly.
- Be a culture carrier: your attitude and energy set the tone for those around you.
  Show up with the curiosity, positivity, and high standards you want to see in the team.

## What You Bring

- 6+ years of professional software development experience; 3+ years in a senior engineering role preferred.
- Experience building or supporting financial or transaction-processing systems such as payments, billing, general ledger, accounts receivable and payable, or reconciliation.
- Advanced SQL and relational database skills, preferably on Microsoft SQL Server.
- Proficiency in TypeScript and Node.js, including asynchronous programming; this is a core, day-to-day part of the role.
- Experience designing and consuming SOAP and REST APIs for system-to-system integration.
- A working understanding of real estate, financial, and accounting concepts.
- Experience with Yardi or another property management system or ERP (such as Oracle, SAP, or NetSuite) is a strong plus.
- Demonstrated ability to design and architect solutions for complex, distributed systems.
- A track record of mentoring engineers and raising the quality of those around you.
- A commitment to continuous improvement in your own craft, your team's capabilities, and the engineering practices around you.
- Strong communication skills (written and verbal) with the ability to engage both technical and non-technical audiences, including executives.
- Experience collaborating across teams and working through technical disagreements constructively.
- Hands-on experience with cloud platforms, CI/CD practices, and modern iterative software delivery.
- A degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.

## Our Stack

Our Financial Systems team works with Yardi, Microsoft SQL Server, TypeScript, and Node.js, integrating through SOAP and REST APIs.
Across engineering, teams also build with React, PostgreSQL, and AWS, including Lambda, DynamoDB, and RDS.
We practice CI/CD, favor infrastructure as code, and build event-driven architectures.
Experience with these is a strong plus, though we care more about depth of thinking, adaptability, and the drive to keep learning than familiarity with any specific tool.
