## About the Role

Invitation Homes set out to solve a real problem: home leasing options were limited and the experience for residents was inconsistent.
Today we're the nation's largest single-family home rental company, with 100,000+ homes.
Our teams build the technology at the center of that solution: personalized resident experiences, service tools, and the operational systems that residents and associates depend on every day.
When we ship, people feel it.

Our technology organization takes that responsibility seriously.
That means collaborative teams, high standards, and real investment in the people around you.
You own the architecture practice that keeps those teams pointed in the same direction.
You set our architecture strategy and make sure it shows up in what we build.
You'll help sustain a culture of code as craft, where teams share principles, reuse proven patterns, and deploy quickly and safely.
This is an individual contributor role today, and your influence comes from expertise and relationships.
As the practice grows, you may build a team, so we want someone who can lead both ways.
Staying close to the technical details without taking decisions away from the teams is the constant tension of the job.
You report to the Senior Vice President (SVP) of Engineering and work closely with the Chief Technology Officer (CTO).

## What You'll Do

**Technical Leadership**

- Own the architecture strategy: set the direction, refine it as the business changes, and realize it by working across all our engineering teams.
- Run the Solution Architecture practice, so teams design together and build to the principles of our architecture strategy.
- Set architecture goals, track execution across teams, and hold the bar on code quality and deployment speed.
- Build security in from the start, with the security team's expertise at every stage of the software development lifecycle.
- Evaluate, recommend, and manage technologies, from open-source tools to vendor relationships.
- Help manage the technology and Engineering budgets through the annual budgeting process.

**Knowledge & Standards**

- Turn short- and long-range strategy into enterprise architecture, then into the design of the systems we build now and next.
- Design enterprise application programming interfaces (APIs) and messaging by breaking complex problems into logical domains.
- Apply enterprise integration architecture patterns and know when each one fits.
- Understand the major architectural styles (layered, monolithic, client-server, component-based, etc.) well enough to choose among them and explain the trade-offs.
- Stay a polyglot: keep up with the modern languages and frameworks our teams use or might adopt, such as TypeScript, Node.js, React, Go, Java, Swift, Kotlin, .NET, and SQL.
- Record the decisions that matter in Architecture Decision Records so the reasoning outlives the people who made it.

**Communication & Collaboration**

- Learn the business and its strategy, then communicate a vision for how Engineering helps execute it.
- Align with the SVP of Engineering, the CTO, and other senior leaders on priorities, scope, requirements, and change management for shared initiatives.
- Collaborate and negotiate with peers inside and outside Engineering, and make the tough call when one is needed.
- Get engineers talking across teams. Share what you learn, and help them see past their own area so they decide better.
- Write and speak clearly.

**Leadership & Culture**

- Shape how the Engineering organization builds, and coach its leaders and senior engineers on both the technical and management tracks.
- Run the Technology Architecture Guild with other technology leaders. It brings together representatives from across engineering to build and maintain strong engineering principles.
- Build the technical, operational, and cultural structures that let teams scale.
- Grow the practice when it needs people: hire, develop, and coach architects and engineers, and manage them directly if the role becomes a team.

## What You Bring

- 15+ years of experience in software development and architecture.
- Experience managing engineers or engineering managers and scaling teams, with the judgment to know when a team is the right answer.
- A track record of coaching and mentoring engineering leaders on both technical and management tracks.
- Proven experience building enterprise APIs and messaging solutions.
- Led architecture, API, or platform work that other engineering teams build on, through a team or through influence.
- A background running an architecture practice or review process, including working with security teams to build security into design.
- Deep, hands-on knowledge of Amazon Web Services (AWS), infrastructure as code, and modern continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) practices.
- A history of owning technology evaluation and vendor decisions, and taking part in budgeting.
- Clear written and verbal communication, with strong public speaking a plus.
- A degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

## Our Stack

Our core AWS services include Lambda, DynamoDB, and RDS.
Our engineering teams build with TypeScript, Node.js, React, PostgreSQL, and Salesforce.
We practice CI/CD, favor infrastructure as code, and build on REST APIs and event-driven architectures.
Familiarity with that stack is helpful but not required.
We care more about depth of thinking, adaptability, and the drive to keep learning than familiarity with any specific tool.
