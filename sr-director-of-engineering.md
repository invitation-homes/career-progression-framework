[Job Description](job-descriptions/sr-director-of-engineering)

As a Sr. Director of Engineering, you set the strategic direction for engineering at the company level.
You tie technology and organizational decisions closely to long-term business outcomes.
You are a senior leader who partners with the VP of Engineering and executive stakeholders to shape the culture, capability, and trajectory of the engineering organization.
Your focus shifts from leading an operating group to leading across the engineering department.
You influence the company by designing the systems, structures, and people strategies that allow engineering to scale and thrive.

## Impact

- Drive company-level technical strategy, shaping multi-year roadmaps that align engineering investments with business priorities and long-term growth objectives.
- Own organizational design decisions that improve how engineering teams are structured, staffed, and operated at scale.
- Identify and address systemic risks, process gaps, and capability shortfalls before they constrain delivery or culture.
- Represent engineering in cross-functional executive partnerships, making sure technology is a strategic enabler across the organization.
- Make build, buy, and partner decisions on AI across the department, and weigh them against cost, risk, and lock-in.
- Own governance for AI in engineering: security, resident data privacy, prompt-injection and agent-permission boundaries, auditability, and change control (CAB, Change Approval Board) for agent-initiated changes.
- Own the engineering budget across the department, including AI spend such as tokens, and advocate for investment in headcount, vendors, tooling, and technology platforms aligned with company strategy.

## Reach

- Leadership Reach
  - Department

- Technical Reach
  - Company

Your leadership spans the engineering department, and your influence extends across the entire company.
You shape how engineering is perceived, prioritized, and resourced at the executive level.
You build trust and alignment with senior leaders across Product, Design, Finance, Business Operations, and beyond, so engineering is a valued and visible partner in company-wide decisions.

## Knowledge

- Maintain deep technical credibility by understanding architectural trade-offs, emerging technology trends, and how to evaluate them against business context.
- Understand how human-agent work changes organizational design: pod composition, review capacity, and the cost and risk of autonomy at scale.
- Develop and apply knowledge of organizational design, talent strategy, and team dynamics to build structures that scale effectively.
- Understand the business deeply enough to translate company goals into engineering strategy, and engineering constraints into language executives can act on.
- Stay connected to the external engineering community and industry trends to keep the organization competitive and informed.

## Communication

- Operate as a clear, trusted voice at the executive level, communicating strategy, trade-offs, risks, and results with confidence and precision.
- Connect technical decisions to business impact in ways that build credibility and earn organizational trust.
- Create alignment across leadership and engineering through transparent, consistent, and timely communication.
- Champion a communication culture within engineering that cascades clarity from strategy to execution at every level.
- Bring dissenting views into executive decisions when engineering's perspective calls for it, then commit fully and lead the department in carrying them out.
- Partner with Sr. Principal Engineers, your counterparts on the Professional Track, so organizational decisions and technical strategy reinforce each other, including the operating model for AI (artificial intelligence) across engineering.
- Own the outcome when AI-assisted work fails: "the agent wrote it" is never an explanation.

## Leadership

- Coach and develop Directors and senior engineering leaders, building a strong bench of technical and organizational leadership across engineering.
- Own the talent strategy for engineering, including hiring, developing, and retaining high-performing leaders and teams at scale.
  Hire for judgment, protect the junior pipeline as agents automate entry-level work, and never measure people by AI usage or output volume.
- Set the cultural tone for engineering at the company level, modeling strategic thinking, accountability, empathy, curiosity, and continuous improvement.
- Partner with the VP of Engineering and CIO to shape the long-term vision for engineering and contribute to the broader technology leadership agenda.
- Serve as a steady, decisive leader in complex situations, resolving organizational conflicts, working through ambiguity, and enabling teams to move forward with clarity and confidence.
- Maintain a bias to action at the department level: make timely calls on structure, investment, and direction, and keep engineering moving while longer-term strategy takes shape.
- Champion automation as a department-wide priority: invest in the platforms, agent guardrails, and evals that keep manual toil from accumulating as engineering scales.
  Verification is the bottleneck, so fund it.
